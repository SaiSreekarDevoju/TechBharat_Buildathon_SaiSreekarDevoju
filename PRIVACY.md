# Privacy Policy

Lumen ("Understand what's on your screen") is designed with privacy by default:

1. **No Background Scraping:** Content is read ONLY when the user explicitly clicks the Lumen extension icon, presses `Alt+Shift+S`, or selects a capture action.
2. **Explicit Data Transmission:** Captured page text/screenshots are transmitted solely to process the user's requested summary or follow-up question.
3. **Sensitive Pattern Redaction:** Privacy mode enables local pre-transmission redaction of emails, phone numbers, API keys, credentials, credit card numbers, PAN, Aadhaar, and IP addresses.
4. **Local Storage:** Summary history is stored strictly on your local browser device (`chrome.storage.local`). You can view, search, or clear history at any time.
5. **No Analytics Tracking:** Page contents and user queries are never sent to third-party analytics services.
